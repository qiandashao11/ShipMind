# Starlight Tech Employee Handbook (RAG demo material)

## Annual leave requests

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees must submit a request in the OA system at least 3 working days in advance, to be approved by their direct manager. Leave of more than 5 consecutive days also requires a second approval from the department head.

Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next calendar year, but must be used before March 31 of that year; after that it expires automatically. The annual leave balance can be viewed on the "Attendance & Leave" page in the OA system.

## Remote work

Employees may request up to 2 days of remote work per week. Requests must be submitted before 18:00 on the previous working day and include the work location, the day's tasks and contact hours. Roles involving customer sites, production releases or operations in secure server rooms are not covered by the standard remote work policy.

## Production incident response

P0 incidents require the on-call engineer to respond within 5 minutes and set up an incident channel within 15 minutes. Progress must be shared every 30 minutes while the incident is being handled. A postmortem document must be completed within 2 working days after service is restored; the postmortem owner is appointed by the Incident Commander for that incident.

## Expense reimbursement

Individual expenses up to CNY 5,000 are approved by the direct manager; expenses above CNY 5,000 also require department head approval. Invoices and payment receipts must be uploaded to the finance system within 30 days of the expense. The travel lodging limit is CNY 600 per night in tier-1 cities and CNY 450 per night in other cities.
